Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

ReviewLoom AI is operated by Bold Forge, LLC, Surprise, Arizona, USA.

What we collect

Review text you paste is sent to our server to generate replies and may be processed by a third-party AI provider. For anonymous and free users we do not store review text. If you create an account we store your email address, sign-in times, your plan, and a daily usage count. If you are a Pro member we also store your saved business profile (business name, type, default tone and length) and your reply history (the reviews you pasted and the replies generated, with dates) so you can see them on your account page. We keep basic server logs and a per-IP daily usage counter to enforce free limits. If you subscribe to our newsletter we store your email address and signup date.

Service providers

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we store your Stripe customer ID and subscription status. Sign-in and account emails are sent through Resend. Data is hosted with our cloud hosting provider (Render). Review text may be sent to an AI provider (such as xAI) to write replies.

Cookies

When you sign in we set a single secure, httpOnly cookie to keep you signed in for up to 30 days. We do not use advertising cookies.

How we use it

To provide the service, manage your account and subscription, prevent abuse, send sign-in and account emails, and send newsletters you asked for. We do not sell personal information. You can unsubscribe, cancel Pro from your account page, or request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.
