How to respond to a 4-star review

A practical guide for small-business owners · Updated November 9, 2026

A 4-star review is a happy customer who noticed one small thing, or who just doesn't hand out perfect scores. It's easy to skip these because nothing seems wrong, but a short, specific reply is one of the cheapest ways to turn a good visit into a regular customer. It also shows people reading your profile that you pay attention to the details. For general advice, start with how to respond to Google reviews.

The short version

  • Thank them by name, warmly.
  • Echo what they loved, using their words where you can.
  • Acknowledge the suggestion in one line if they made one, without sounding defensive.
  • Skip the hunt for problems if they didn't mention any.
  • Invite them back, ideally with something specific to try next time.

Why 4-star reviews are worth a reply

Many shoppers trust a profile with plenty of 4-star reviews more than one with nothing but perfect scores, because it looks real. Your replies under those reviews show how you take friendly feedback. A reviewer who gets a thoughtful thank-you is more likely to come back, recommend you, and leave another review later.

1. Great experience, one small issue

Thanks so much, Maria! We're thrilled you loved the carne asada plate and that Luis took such good care of your table. You're right that parking can be tight on Friday nights. There's free overflow parking behind the bank next door after 6, and we'll add a note about it to our Google profile. See you next time!

Why it works: it leads with what they loved, treats the small complaint as useful, and gives a practical fix that helps every future reader too.

2. Four stars with no complaint at all

Thank you, Dan! We're glad the new brakes feel great and that we got you back on the road the same day. We appreciate you trusting us with your truck, and we'll be here whenever it needs anything.

Why it works: it doesn't ask why they held back a star. Some people never give five, and treating a 4-star like a problem can come across as needy.

3. Good service, price felt high

Thanks for the kind words about the cleaning, Erin, and we're happy Dr. Patel made you feel comfortable. We hear you on the cost. Our front desk can always walk through your insurance coverage before your next visit so there are no surprises.

Why it works: it doesn't defend the price. It offers clarity, which is usually what the customer actually wanted.

4. A suggestion for something new

Thank you, Chris! We love hearing that Biscuit came home looking so fluffy. Online booking is a great idea, and it's on our list for this winter. Until then, you can text us at (555) 123-4567 anytime to grab a spot.

Why it works: it takes the idea seriously, shares an honest timeline only because there is one, and offers a workaround now.

5. A short review like "Pretty good"

Thanks for stopping by, Taylor! We're glad you had a good visit, and we'd love to make the next one even better. Try the seasonal pumpkin latte next time.

Why it works: it matches the length of the review, stays upbeat, and gives a friendly reason to return.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Asking "What could we have done to earn 5 stars?" when they didn't mention a problem.
  • Asking them to change their rating.
  • Explaining or defending the small issue at length. One line is enough.
  • Copying the same "Thanks for the 5 stars!" style reply you use for perfect reviews, which can read like you didn't actually read it.
  • Promising a fix you aren't actually planning to make.

What to do after you reply

The small notes in 4-star reviews, such as parking, wait times, or booking, are often the easiest wins on your whole profile. Keep a running list and fix the ones that come up more than once. Questions owners ask

Should I respond to 4-star reviews?

Yes. A 4-star review comes from a happy customer who noticed one thing that could be better. Replying thanks them, shows future customers you read feedback, and often turns a good visit into a loyal regular.

Why didn't they give 5 stars?

Sometimes there is a small issue, like a wait or a price, and sometimes the reviewer simply never gives 5 stars. If they mention something specific, address it briefly. If they don't, just thank them and don't go looking for a problem.

Should I ask a 4-star reviewer to update to 5 stars?

No. Asking for a higher rating feels pushy and can break review platform rules. Thank them, act on any suggestion, and let a great next visit speak for itself.

How long should a reply to a 4-star review be?

Two to four sentences is plenty. Thank them by name, mention one specific thing they liked, acknowledge any suggestion in one line, and invite them back.
