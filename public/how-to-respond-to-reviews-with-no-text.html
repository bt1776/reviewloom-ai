How to respond to reviews with no text

A practical guide for small-business owners · Updated November 2, 2026

Google lets customers leave a star rating without writing anything. These star-only reviews are common, and they're easy to skip because there's nothing to respond to. But they still count toward your rating, they still show on your profile, and a short reply still tells future customers that you pay attention. This guide covers when to reply, what to say for each rating, and examples you can adapt. For reviews with a full comment, read how to respond to Google reviews.

Should you reply at all?

Yes, when you have the time. A star-only review gives you very little to work with, so the reply is short, but it does three useful things:

  • It shows people browsing your profile that every customer gets a response, not only the ones who wrote a paragraph.
  • On a low rating, it opens a door for the customer to tell you what went wrong so you can fix it.
  • It gives you a natural place to mention your business name and what you do, in your own words.

If you're short on time, prioritize 1, 2, and 3 star ratings first. Those are the ones future customers read most closely.

A simple formula

  1. Thank them, by first name if the review shows one.
  2. Match the tone to the rating: warm for 4 and 5 stars, concerned for 1 to 3 stars.
  3. For a low rating, admit you don't know the details and give a direct way to reach you.
  4. Keep it to two or three sentences. A long reply to a blank review looks odd.

Don't guess what happened, and don't try to identify the customer in public. If you think you know who it was, say so privately, not in the reply.

1. 5-star rating, no comment

Thanks so much for the 5 stars, Jordan! We're glad you chose Desert Bloom Landscaping, and we'd love to help with your next project.

Why it works: it's personal, uses the business name naturally, and invites them back without overdoing it.

Thank you for the great rating! It means a lot to our small team. See you next time.

Why it works: when there's no name, a short, sincere thank-you is enough. Vary the wording from review to review so your replies don't look copied.

2. 4-star rating, no comment

Thanks for the 4 stars, Priya! We're always working to earn that fifth one, so if there's anything we could have done better, we'd love to hear it at hello@yourbusiness.com.

Why it works: it thanks them and gently asks what would make it a 5, which can surface useful feedback.

3. 3-star rating, no comment

Thank you for taking the time to rate us, Sam. It sounds like your visit was just okay, and we'd really like to know why. Could you give us a call at (555) 123-4567 so we can make the next one better?

Why it works: it treats a middle rating as a missed chance to impress, not an attack, and moves the conversation offline.

4. 1 or 2 star rating, no comment

We're sorry your experience didn't meet your expectations, Alex. We'd like to understand what happened and make it right. Please reach out to our manager, Dana, at (555) 123-4567 or dana@yourbusiness.com.

Why it works: it stays calm, doesn't argue or guess, and names a real person and contact so the reviewer knows the offer is genuine. Readers mostly judge you on how you respond to the worst reviews.

5. Low rating from someone you can't find in your records

Thank you for the rating. We couldn't find a visit under this name, and we'd like to learn more about what happened. Please contact us at (555) 123-4567 so we can look into it.

Why it works: it's polite and factual without accusing anyone. If you believe the review is fake or breaks Google's rules, you can also report it. See how to respond to fake reviews.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Pasting the exact same line under every star-only review. It reads as automated.
  • Asking a low-star reviewer to change or remove their rating, or offering something in exchange for that.
  • Guessing out loud about who the customer is or what went wrong.
  • Writing a long reply. Two or three sentences fits a review with no text.

Questions owners ask

Should I respond to a Google review that has no text?

Yes, when you can. A short, personal reply shows future customers that you read every review, and for a low rating it invites the customer to tell you what went wrong.

What do I say to a 5-star review with no comment?

Thank the person by name, mention your business or something they likely used, and invite them back. Two or three sentences is plenty.

How do I reply to a 1-star review with no explanation?

Stay calm, say you are sorry their experience fell short, admit you don't know the details, and give a direct way to reach you, such as a phone number or email, so you can fix it privately.

Can I ask the reviewer to explain their rating?

Yes, politely. Invite them to contact you or share more so you can improve, but don't pressure them or ask them to change or remove the review.
