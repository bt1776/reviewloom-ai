Frequently asked questions

What is a Google review link?

It is a direct link that opens the Google review box for your business, so customers can leave a review in one tap instead of searching for you on Maps.

How do I find my Google Place ID?

Use Google's free Place ID Finder: search your business name, click your listing on the map, and copy the Place ID shown (it usually starts with ChIJ). You can also paste a Google Maps link that contains place_id.

Is this Google review link generator free?

Yes. ReviewLoom AI's review link generator is free, needs no signup, and creates your link, QR code, and request templates right in your browser.

Can I print the QR code?

Yes. Download the PNG and add it to receipts, counter signs, invoices, or business cards. Customers scan it to open your review form.

Can I offer a discount for a Google review?

No. Google doesn't allow incentives for reviews. Ask every customer for honest feedback and leave it at that.
