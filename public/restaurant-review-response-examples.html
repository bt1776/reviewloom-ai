Restaurant review response examples

Replies for Google and Yelp reviews, from slow nights to 5-star praise · Updated October 23, 2026

Restaurants get more reviews than almost any other small business, and many of them land in the middle of a dinner rush. Below are ten common restaurant reviews with a reply for each and a short note on why it works. Change the names and details so each reply sounds like you, and don’t paste the same text on every review. For the general method, read how to respond to Google reviews.

A quick formula for restaurant replies

  1. Thank the guest and mention something specific they said, such as a dish, a server, or the occasion.
  2. If something went wrong, apologize once and say what you’re doing about it.
  3. Move refunds, comps, and details to a private conversation by phone or email.
  4. Sign with a real name and role, like “Maria, General Manager,” so it feels personal.

1. Slow service on a busy night

Sample review: “Food was good but we waited 50 minutes for our entrées and nobody checked on us.” 3 stars.

Thank you for coming in and for the kind words about the food. A 50-minute wait with no check-in isn’t the experience we want for anyone, and I’m sorry. We’ve talked with the team about checking on tables when the kitchen is backed up. I hope you’ll give us another try, and please ask for me when you do. – Maria, General Manager

Why it works: It thanks them for the praise, owns the wait without excuses, and names a specific change.

2. Cold or undercooked food

Sample review: “My burger came out cold and the fries were soggy.” 2 stars.

We’re sorry your burger and fries weren’t hot and fresh. That’s not our standard, and we’d have gladly remade them on the spot. Please reach out to us at (555) 123-4567 so we can make it up to you on your next visit.

Why it works: It apologizes once, mentions the fix a server could have made, and moves the make-good offer to a private conversation.

3. Wrong takeout or delivery order

Sample review: “Ordered online and got someone else’s order. Called and nobody answered.” 1 star.

I’m really sorry your order was wrong and that you couldn’t reach us. That’s two misses in one night. We’re adding a second check on takeout bags and making sure the phone is covered during the rush. Please email us at hello@example.com with your order number and we’ll refund it.

Why it works: It names both problems, explains the fix in one sentence, and gives a clear next step.

4. Rude server or host

Sample review: “The host was rude and acted like we were a bother.” 2 stars.

Thank you for telling us. Everyone who walks in should feel welcome, and I’m sorry you didn’t. I’ve shared your feedback with our front-of-house team. If you’re willing, please call me at (555) 123-4567 so I can hear more about your visit.

Why it works: It doesn’t throw the employee under the bus in public, and it invites the guest to share details privately.

5. Billing or tip mistake

Sample review: “We were charged for two drinks we never ordered.” 2 stars.

We’re sorry about the extra charges on your bill. Please contact us at hello@example.com with the date and the last four digits of your card, and we’ll refund the difference right away.

Why it works: Money complaints need a fast, concrete fix. It asks only for what’s needed to find the charge, and nothing sensitive.

6. Food safety or illness worry

Sample review: “I got sick after eating here last night.” 1 star.

We’re very sorry you’re not feeling well, and we take this seriously. We’re reviewing that night with our kitchen team. Please contact our manager directly at (555) 123-4567 with what you ordered and when, so we can look into it properly.

Why it works: It’s calm and serious, doesn’t argue about the cause, and moves the details offline.

7. Prices too high

Sample review: “Way too expensive for the portion size.” 3 stars.

Thank you for the honest feedback. We use local ingredients and make everything from scratch, which is reflected in our prices, but we hear you on portions and we’ve shared this with our chef. We also have a weekday lunch menu if you’d like to visit at a lower price point.

Why it works: It explains the value without being defensive and offers a real alternative.

8. Mixed review with one complaint

Sample review: “Amazing tacos, but the music was so loud we couldn’t talk.” 4 stars.

Thank you, we’re so glad you loved the tacos! You’re right that it gets loud on weekend nights. We’ve turned the volume down a notch, and our patio is a little quieter if you’d like a calmer spot next time.

Why it works: It leads with thanks, agrees with the fair point, and gives a helpful tip.

9. Glowing 5-star review

Sample review: “Best anniversary dinner ever. Jamie was the best server we’ve had anywhere.” 5 stars.

Happy anniversary, and thank you for celebrating with us! We’ll make sure Jamie sees this, because it will make their week. We hope to see you back for many more celebrations.

Why it works: It’s warm, specific, and passes the praise to the staff member by name, which the reviewer already used.

10. Short 5-star review with no text

Sample review: No text, 5 stars.

Thank you for the 5 stars! We’re glad you enjoyed your visit and hope to see you again soon.

Why it works: Short is fine here. A quick thank-you shows readers the owner reads every review.

Mistakes restaurants make in review replies

  • Blaming the guest, the delivery app, or a new hire in public.
  • Offering free meals in the public reply, which can invite more complaints.
  • Copying the same “Thanks for your feedback!” on every review, which readers notice.
  • Arguing about food safety or sharing a guest’s order details in public.

Questions restaurant owners ask

How quickly should a restaurant respond to reviews?

Within a day or two is a good goal. A prompt reply shows guests that someone is paying attention, and it is easier to fix a problem while the visit is still fresh.

Should a restaurant respond to every review?

Reply to every negative or mixed review and as many positive ones as you can. Short, specific thanks on 5-star reviews encourage repeat visits and show new guests that the owner is involved.

Should I offer a free meal in a review reply?

Make offers privately, not in the public reply. Invite the guest to contact you, then decide on a refund or a return visit. Public offers can invite people to complain just to get a free meal.

How do I respond to a food safety complaint?

Take it seriously, thank the guest, say you are looking into it with your kitchen team, and ask them to contact a manager directly with the date and what they ordered. Do not argue about the cause in public.
