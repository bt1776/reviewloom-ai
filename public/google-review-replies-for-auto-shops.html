Frequently asked questions

Why not just use ChatGPT?

You can. ReviewLoom AI is built only for review replies, so you skip writing a prompt every time. It follows review-reply rules by default: it thanks the customer, references what they said, apologizes and moves complaints offline, avoids public discounts, and does not invent facts. You get three options in your chosen tone and length, and Pro saves your shop details and past replies.

Is it free?

Yes. You can generate replies free with no account, and a free account gives you 5 a day. Pro is unlimited. The first 25 Pro members pay $5/month for life with code FOUNDER, which is applied at checkout while spots last. The normal price is $9/month.

Does ReviewLoom AI post replies to Google for me?

No. You copy the reply you like and paste it into your Google Business Profile. Nothing is posted without you.

Will the reply admit fault or offer free work?

The replies are written to stay calm and fair, apologize for the experience, and invite the customer to call or come in, without promising refunds or free repairs in public. Always read and edit a reply before you post it.

Can I mention my shop name and city?

Yes. Add your shop name, the service, and your town, and ReviewLoom AI works them into the reply naturally, which can help local search.
