What makes a good review reply

  • Thank the reviewer and mention a specific detail from what they wrote.
  • On negative reviews, apologize once, take ownership, and offer a phone number or email.
  • Stay calm. Never blame the customer or argue the facts in public.
  • Keep it to 3-5 sentences and sign with a real name and role.
  • Make refunds and make-goods privately, not in the reply.
