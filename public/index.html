Frequently asked questions

Is the review reply generator free?

Yes. Try 2 replies a day with no signup, or get 5 free replies a day with a free account (just your email, no password). Pro is normally $9/month for unlimited replies; the first 25 Pro users get a founder price of $5/month for life with code FOUNDER.

Should I reply to negative Google reviews?

Yes. A calm, apologetic reply that takes ownership and invites the customer to continue offline shows future customers you care, and can help your local reputation.

Does replying to reviews help local SEO?

Google says responding to reviews shows you value customers. Active, thoughtful responses are widely considered a positive signal for your Google Business Profile.

Will the replies sound robotic?

No. Replies reference details from the review and match the tone you choose: warm, professional, upbeat, or apologetic. Always read and tweak before posting.

Do you store my reviews?

Only for Pro members, so you can see your reply history. For free and anonymous users, reviews are processed to generate replies and are not saved.
