How to respond to Facebook reviews

A practical guide for small-business owners · Updated October 27, 2026

Facebook reviews show up on your business Page, where friends of the reviewer and anyone checking you out can see them. Replying well turns a review into a small ad for how you treat customers. This guide covers how Facebook reviews work, a simple reply formula, and examples you can adapt. For other platforms, read how to respond to Google reviews and how to respond to Yelp reviews.

How Facebook reviews work

Facebook calls them recommendations. Instead of a star rating, people say whether they recommend your business and add a comment, and your Page shows an overall share of people who recommend you. You reply by commenting on the recommendation while acting as your Page, so the reply appears under your business name, not your personal profile.

You can't delete someone’s recommendation, but you can report one that breaks Facebook’s Community Standards. Facebook also lets admins turn reviews off for a Page, but that hides the good ones too, so replying is usually the better move.

A simple formula

  1. Thank them by name.
  2. Mention something specific from what they wrote.
  3. For a complaint, own what’s fair and say what you’re doing about it.
  4. Invite them back, or ask them to message your Page to sort out private details.

Keep it short. On Facebook, people skim comments on their phones, and two or three warm sentences beat a long paragraph.

1. Positive recommendation

Sample review: “Recommends. Jake fixed our AC the same day we called, and the price was exactly what he quoted.”

Thank you, Sarah! Same-day fixes are what Jake lives for, and we’ll make sure he sees this. Stay cool out there, and we’re here whenever you need us.

Why it works: it uses her name, credits the employee, and sounds like a person rather than a template.

2. Short positive recommendation with no details

Sample review: “Recommends. Great place!”

Thanks so much for the recommendation, Tom! We’re glad you had a good visit and hope to see you again soon.

Why it works: a quick, friendly thank-you is all a short review needs.

3. Mixed recommendation

Sample review: “Recommends, but the online booking said 10am and nobody was there until 10:30.”

Thanks for recommending us, Priya, and sorry about the wait. That booking mix-up is on us, and we’ve fixed the schedule sync so online times match the shop. We appreciate your patience and hope your next visit is right on time.

Why it works: it thanks them for the recommendation and fixes the problem in public, so other readers trust the booking system.

4. Doesn’t recommend, with a specific complaint

Sample review: “Doesn’t recommend. My cake order was wrong and nobody called me back.”

We’re really sorry, Dana. A wrong order and no callback isn’t the experience we want for anyone. Please send our Page a message with your order date so we can make this right.

Why it works: the public reply shows you care, and moving to Messenger keeps order details private.

5. Doesn’t recommend, and you can’t find them

Sample review: “Doesn’t recommend. Terrible service.” You have no record of this person.

Thanks for letting us know. We couldn’t find your visit in our records, and we’d like to understand what happened. Please send us a message so we can look into it.

Why it works: it stays calm and invites a conversation without accusing anyone. If it looks like spam or breaks the rules, report it too. See how to respond to fake reviews for more.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Replying from your personal profile instead of the Page.
  • Arguing in the comments. Friends of the reviewer can see the thread too.
  • Posting a customer’s order, bill, or contact details publicly.
  • Offering a discount in exchange for changing a review.

Questions owners ask

Can I delete a negative Facebook review?

No, a Page can't delete someone's recommendation. You can report one that you believe breaks Facebook's Community Standards, and you can reply publicly so other people see your side.

Can I turn off reviews on my Facebook Page?

Facebook lets Page admins turn reviews off in the Page settings. That hides recommendations from your Page, but it also removes the positive ones, so most businesses are better off leaving them on and replying.

Should I reply to every Facebook recommendation?

Try to reply to every negative or mixed one and as many positive ones as you can. A short, personal thank-you shows people browsing your Page that a real person is listening.

Should I move the conversation to Messenger?

For problems that involve private details like an order, a bill, or a booking, reply publicly first so people can see you responded, then invite the reviewer to message your Page so you can sort out the details privately.
