How to respond to Yelp reviews

A practical guide for small-business owners · Updated October 26, 2026

Yelp works a little differently from Google. You can answer a review in public, message the reviewer privately, or both, and choosing well matters. This guide explains the options, gives you a simple formula, and includes Yelp reply examples you can adapt. For Google, read how to respond to Google reviews.

Before you start

You reply from a claimed business page using Yelp for Business, on the website or the app. Yelp requires an approved profile photo and an approved business account name before you can respond, so set those up first. Yelp’s own steps are in How do I respond to reviews of my business?

Public comment or direct message?

  • Public comment. It appears under the review and anyone viewing your page can read it. Use it for almost every review, because you’re really writing for future customers.
  • Direct message. Only you and the reviewer see it. Use it to sort out private details like an order number, a refund, or a bill.
  • Thank button. For 4 and 5 star reviews, Yelp offers a quick private thank-you note. It’s fine in a pinch, but a personal public comment is better.

For critical reviews, Yelp recommends posting a public comment first and then following up by direct message if you need to, so other customers can see you took the problem seriously.

A simple formula

  1. Thank the reviewer for the feedback.
  2. Mention a specific detail from their review, such as their name or what they ordered.
  3. Address any concern, take responsibility where it’s fair, and say what you’re doing about it.
  4. Invite them back, or offer a way to keep talking privately.

Take a minute before you reply to a harsh review. Your comment represents your business long after the moment passes, and you can always edit or delete it later from the three-dot menu.

1. Glowing 5-star review

Sample review: “Best tacos in town. Maria at the counter remembered our order from last time!” 5 stars.

Thank you so much! We’ll make sure Maria hears this, she loves our regulars. Next time, try the carnitas special on Fridays. See you soon!

Why it works: it names the detail they loved and gives them a reason to come back. Yelp also has a quick Thank button for 4 and 5 star reviews, but a short personal comment does more.

2. Mixed 3-star review

Sample review: “Food was great but we waited 40 minutes for a table even with a reservation.” 3 stars.

Thanks for the honest review, and we’re glad you enjoyed the food. A 40-minute wait with a reservation isn’t okay, and we’re sorry. We’ve changed how we space Saturday bookings so tables turn on time. We hope you’ll give us another try.

Why it works: it accepts the fair criticism and tells readers what changed, without excuses.

3. Angry 1-star review about a specific problem

Sample review: “They charged me for work they never did. Never again.” 1 star.

We’re sorry to read this, and we want to get it right. Billing mistakes are serious to us. I’ve sent you a direct message so we can look at your invoice together and fix anything that’s wrong.

Why it works: the public comment shows you take it seriously, and the direct message moves the private details, like the invoice, out of public view.

4. Review that is mostly about a policy

Sample review: “They wouldn’t let me bring my own wine. Ridiculous.” 2 stars.

Thanks for the feedback, and sorry for the frustration. Our liquor license doesn’t allow outside alcohol, so we can’t make exceptions, but we do offer a wine list with bottles under $30. We’d love to have you back.

Why it works: it explains the reason once, calmly, so future customers understand the policy before they visit.

5. Review you can’t match to a customer

Sample review: “Rude staff and dirty bathrooms.” 1 star, and you can’t find the visit.

Thank you for letting us know. We couldn’t match this review to a visit in our records, and we’d like to learn more. I’ve sent you a direct message, or you can reach us at (555) 123-4567.

Why it works: it stays polite, doesn’t accuse anyone, and invites a real conversation. If the review breaks Yelp’s guidelines, report it too. For more on suspected fakes, see how to respond to fake reviews.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Arguing point by point in public. Readers side with the calmer voice.
  • Copying the same reply onto every review. It reads as automated and loses trust.
  • Sharing a customer’s private details to prove you’re right.
  • Offering discounts or freebies in exchange for changing or removing a review.

Yelp also publishes a list of DOs and DON’Ts for business owners that’s worth a read.

Questions owners ask

Should I respond to Yelp reviews publicly or with a direct message?

Start with a public comment for most reviews, especially critical ones, so future customers can see how you handle problems. Add a direct message when you need to discuss details privately, such as an order number or a refund.

Do I need anything set up before I can reply on Yelp?

You need a claimed Yelp business page, and Yelp requires an approved profile photo and an approved business account name before you can respond to reviews.

Can I get a Yelp review removed?

You can report a review that you believe breaks Yelp's content guidelines. Yelp's moderators review it and email you their decision, and reporting a review does not guarantee it will be removed.

Can I edit my reply after I post it?

Yes. In Yelp for Business, open the three-dot menu next to your response to edit or delete it.
