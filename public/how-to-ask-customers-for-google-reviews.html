How to ask customers for Google reviews

A practical guide for small-business owners · Updated October 28, 2026

Most happy customers never leave a review because nobody asked. A short, well-timed request is the single easiest way to get more Google reviews. This guide covers when to ask, how to get your review link, what Google allows, and templates you can copy. Once reviews start coming in, read how to respond to Google reviews.

When to ask

Ask right after a good moment: the job is finished, the customer says thanks, or the order arrives. Memory is fresh and goodwill is high. Same day is best, and within a couple of days still works well.

Get your Google review link

Google Business Profile gives you a short link that opens the review box directly. Find your Business Profile on Google while signed in as the owner, choose the option to ask for reviews, and copy the link. Put it in texts, emails, receipts, and a QR code at the counter so customers don’t have to search for you.

Stay inside Google’s rules

  • Don’t offer discounts, gifts, or entries into a drawing in exchange for a review.
  • Don’t ask only the customers you think are happy. Google calls that review gating.
  • Don’t write reviews for yourself or have employees and family review you.
  • Don’t set up a review station or tablet in your shop where everyone posts from one device.

Text message template

Hi Maria, thanks for choosing Desert Bloom Landscaping today! If you have a minute, would you share how it went on Google? It really helps a small local business like ours: [your review link]

Why it works: it’s personal, short, and the link takes them straight to the review box.

Email template

Subject: How did we do?

Hi James,

Thanks again for having us out to fix your water heater. We hope everything is running smoothly. If you have a moment, we’d be grateful if you’d leave an honest review on Google. It takes about a minute and helps neighbors find a plumber they can trust.

[your review link]

Thanks,
Luis, Valley Plumbing

Why it works: it reminds them what you did, asks for an honest review rather than a five-star one, and explains why it matters.

In-person script

I’m really glad you’re happy with it. We’re a small shop, and Google reviews make a big difference for us. Would you mind leaving one? This card has a QR code that takes you right there.

Why it works: you ask while they’re smiling, and the card removes every step between the yes and the review.

Friendly reminder template

Hi Maria, just a quick follow-up in case my last message got buried. If you have a minute to share your experience on Google, here’s the link: [your review link]. Either way, thanks for your business!

Why it works: one gentle nudge is fine, and “either way” keeps it pressure-free. Don’t send more than one reminder.

Questions owners ask

Can I offer a discount for a Google review?

No. Google’s policies don’t allow offering money, discounts, or freebies in exchange for reviews, and review incentives can also run into FTC rules. Ask for honest feedback and leave it at that.

Can I ask only happy customers for reviews?

Google calls that review gating and doesn’t allow it. Ask customers in general, not just the ones you expect to leave five stars.

Where do I find my Google review link?

Open your Business Profile on Google (search your business name while signed in, or use Google Business Profile Manager), choose the option to ask for reviews, and copy the short link it gives you.

How many times should I ask?

Ask once, and send at most one friendly reminder a few days later. More than that starts to feel like pressure.

Should I reply to the reviews I get?

Yes. Replying to new reviews, good and bad, shows future customers you’re listening and encourages more people to leave one.
