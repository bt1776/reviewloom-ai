Salon and spa review response examples

For hair salons, barbershops, nail salons, med spas, and massage studios · Updated October 23, 2026

Salon and spa reviews are personal: they’re about how someone looks and feels. A thoughtful reply can turn a bad haircut into a loyal client. Below are nine common salon and spa reviews with replies and why each works. For the general method, read how to respond to Google reviews.

A quick formula for salon or spa replies

  1. Thank the client and mention the service or stylist they named.
  2. For complaints, apologize for how they feel, not just the result, and mention your redo or fix policy in general terms.
  3. Move fixes and refunds to a private message or call.
  4. Sign as the owner or manager, like “Jess, Owner.”

1. Haircut not what they asked for

Sample review: “Asked for a trim and she took off four inches. I’m devastated.” 1 star.

I’m so sorry. Losing more length than you asked for is upsetting, and we understand how much it matters. We offer complimentary adjustments within 7 days, and I’d like to personally help. Please call me at (555) 123-4567. – Jess, Owner

Why it works: It leads with empathy and mentions an existing policy rather than inventing a public freebie.

2. Hair color went wrong

Sample review: “Wanted ash blonde, came out brassy orange.” 2 stars.

Thank you for telling us, and we’re sorry the color wasn’t what you hoped for. Color correction is something we take seriously, and we’d love the chance to get it right. Please reach out at (555) 123-4567 so we can find a time with our senior colorist.

Why it works: It offers a clear path to fix it without blaming the client’s hair history.

3. Kept waiting past appointment

Sample review: “Waited 30 minutes past my appointment time with no apology.” 2 stars.

We’re sorry for the wait and that no one acknowledged it. Your time matters. We’ve updated our front desk process so clients are told right away if a stylist is running behind. – Jess

Why it works: It owns both the wait and the missing apology.

4. Price higher than expected

Sample review: “Was quoted $120 and charged $185 with no explanation.” 2 stars.

We’re sorry for the surprise at checkout. Our team should always confirm any price changes, such as extra product for longer hair, before the service. Please contact me at (555) 123-4567 so we can review it together. – Jess, Owner

Why it works: It explains a common reason without debating the specific bill publicly.

5. Cleanliness concern

Sample review: “The pedicure tubs didn’t look clean. Gross.” 2 stars.

Thank you for raising this. Cleanliness is non-negotiable for us: every tub is disinfected and liner-changed between clients. We’ve shared your feedback with our team to make sure that’s visible and consistent. Please call me with any details at (555) 123-4567.

Why it works: Health concerns need a direct, confident answer about the standard.

6. Massage was too rough or too light

Sample review: “Asked for light pressure and it was painful the whole time.” 2 stars.

We’re sorry. Your comfort should always come first, and your therapist should have checked in and adjusted. We’ve shared this with our team. We’d love to hear more and make it right. Please call (555) 123-4567.

Why it works: It sets the right standard (checking in) and invites a private conversation.

7. Stylist praise

Sample review: “Maria is a magician with curls. Best cut I’ve had in years!” 5 stars.

Thank you! We’ll pass this on to Maria; curls are her favorite. We can’t wait to see you at your next appointment.

Why it works: Specific, warm, and gives credit to the stylist by name.

8. Relaxing spa day

Sample review: “Booked a day package for my birthday. Felt like a vacation.” 5 stars.

Happy birthday, and thank you for celebrating with us! We’re so glad you got a real break. Come back and relax anytime.

Why it works: It reflects the occasion and the feeling they described.

9. Short 5-star review

Sample review: No text, 5 stars. 5 stars.

Thank you for the 5 stars! We appreciate you and hope to see you soon.

Why it works: Short and sweet is fine for no-text reviews.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Blaming the client’s hair, skin, or instructions in public.
  • Posting “before” details or photos the client didn’t share.
  • Offering a free service in the public reply.
  • Letting stylists reply from personal accounts in an emotional moment.

Frequently asked questions

How should a salon respond to a bad haircut review?

Apologize for how the client feels, mention your adjustment or redo policy in general terms, and invite them to call the owner or manager. Avoid blaming their instructions.

Should a salon offer a refund in a review reply?

Make refund or redo offers privately. Publicly, say you would like to make it right and give a direct contact.

Should I respond to positive salon reviews?

Yes. Thank the client and mention the stylist they named. It encourages rebooking and shows new clients your team is appreciated.

Can a med spa respond to reviews about treatments?

Be careful. If you provide medical treatments, avoid confirming the reviewer was a client or discussing their treatment. Keep the reply general and invite them to call.
