How quickly should you respond to Google reviews?

A practical guide for small-business owners · Updated November 12, 2026

Most owners know they should reply to reviews. The harder question is when. Reply too slowly and your profile looks unattended, with complaints sitting there for months. Reply too fast to an upsetting review and you risk posting something you regret. Here's a realistic schedule that a busy owner can actually keep. For what to say, start with how to respond to Google reviews.

The short version

  • Target: one to two business days for every review.
  • Negative reviews first. Same or next business day if you can do it calmly.
  • Positive reviews in batches during a regular weekly reply session.
  • Turn on notifications so new reviews don't slip past you.
  • Old, unanswered reviews? Reply anyway. Late beats never.

Why speed matters

A reply is for two audiences: the customer who wrote the review and every future customer who reads it. A prompt reply tells the reviewer you're listening while the visit is still fresh. It also shows shoppers comparing you with other businesses that someone is paying attention. A profile full of unanswered reviews, especially unanswered complaints, sends the opposite message.

A simple schedule that works

1. Same day: read and sort

When a notification comes in, read it and decide which bucket it goes in: urgent (a complaint, a safety or billing issue, or something factually wrong), normal (a mixed or short review), or easy (a happy customer). You don't have to reply yet.

2. Within one business day: answer complaints

Check your records, talk to the staff involved, then reply calmly. Keep it short, take responsibility for what went wrong, and move the details offline. See how to respond to 1-star reviews and 2-star reviews for examples.

3. Once or twice a week: everything else

Block 15 minutes, for example Monday and Thursday morning, to answer positive, mixed, and star-only reviews in one sitting. Batching keeps it from interrupting your day. Our 4-star, 3-star, and no-text review guides cover those.

Example replies

A quick reply to a complaint (next morning)

Thank you for letting us know, Maria. I'm sorry your order was wrong last night. That's not the experience we want for anyone. I've spoken with the evening team about double-checking to-go orders, and I'd like to make this right. Please call me at (555) 123-4567.

Why it works: it's prompt, calm, specific about the fix, and moves the rest of the conversation offline.

A batched reply to a happy customer

Thanks so much, Kevin! We're glad the new tires made such a difference on your commute. See you at your next rotation.

Why it works: personal, short, and easy to write during a weekly session. More ideas on our positive review examples page.

A late reply to a review from months ago

Thank you for this review, Priya, and I apologize for the slow reply. We're so glad the team took good care of your dog, and we hope to see you both again soon.

Why it works: a brief apology for the delay, then a normal reply. For a full backlog, see how to respond to old reviews.

Catching up on a backlog

  • Start with unanswered negative reviews, newest first.
  • Then answer the most recent positive reviews, since those are what shoppers see first.
  • Work back through older reviews a few at a time each session until you're caught up.
  • Keep each reply specific. Pasting the same line under twenty reviews looks automated.

When not to reply right away

Speed is good, but not at the cost of a calm, accurate reply. Wait if you're angry, if you need to check what actually happened, or if the review might be fake and you plan to report it first (see how to respond to fake reviews). A thoughtful reply tomorrow beats a defensive one in five minutes.

Questions owners ask

How quickly should I respond to a Google review?

Aim for one to two business days. That is fast enough that the customer still remembers the visit and future customers see an active owner, and slow enough that you can reply calmly instead of reacting.

Should I reply to negative reviews faster than positive ones?

Yes, answer negative reviews first. An unanswered complaint is what shoppers notice, and a quick, calm reply can stop the problem from growing. Positive reviews can wait for your regular reply session.

Is it too late to reply to a review from months ago?

No. A late reply is better than none, because future customers read it either way. Acknowledge the delay in a few words and keep it short.

Should I reply right away to an angry review?

Not if you're upset. Take a little time, gather the facts, and reply when you can be calm, ideally the same or next business day. A fast angry reply does more damage than a slightly slower thoughtful one.

How often should I check for new reviews?

Turn on review notifications in your Google Business Profile so you see new reviews as they arrive, then set one or two short reply sessions a week to answer anything still open.
