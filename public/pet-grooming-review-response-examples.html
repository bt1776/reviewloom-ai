Pet grooming and pet services review response examples

For dog groomers, self-serve pet washes, mobile groomers, daycare, and boarding · Updated October 23, 2026

Pet owners treat their dogs and cats like family, so reviews of groomers and pet services are often emotional. A calm, caring reply matters even more when a pet was involved. Below are nine common pet services reviews with replies and why each works. For the general method, read how to respond to Google reviews.

A quick formula for pet services business replies

  1. Thank the owner and use the pet’s name if they shared it.
  2. If a pet was hurt or stressed, lead with concern for the animal, then explain what you are doing.
  3. Move vet bills, refunds, and details to a phone call.
  4. Sign as the owner, like “Sam, Owner.”

1. Pet came home with a nick or cut

Sample review: “Picked up my dog and found a cut on her ear. Nobody told me.” 1 star.

I’m so sorry. First, I hope she’s feeling okay. Any nick, however small, should always be shown to the owner at pickup, and that didn’t happen. We’ve reviewed our pickup check with every groomer. Please call me directly at (555) 123-4567; I want to talk with you about this personally. – Sam, Owner

Why it works: Leads with the pet’s wellbeing, owns the communication failure, and moves the conversation offline.

2. Haircut too short

Sample review: “Asked for a puppy cut and my doodle came back shaved.” 2 stars.

We’re sorry he came home shorter than you wanted. Sometimes matting means a shorter cut is the kindest option, but we should have called you before going shorter. We’ve made that call mandatory. We’d love to talk about a style plan for next time. – Sam

Why it works: It explains a real reason (matting) without hiding behind it and names the fix.

3. Anxious or stressed pet

Sample review: “My cat was terrified when I picked her up.” 2 stars.

Thank you for telling us. Grooming can be stressful for cats, and we never want any pet leaving upset. We offer shorter, quieter appointments for anxious pets, and we’d be glad to talk through options. Please call (555) 123-4567.

Why it works: It shows care and offers a concrete alternative.

4. Long wait or late pickup

Sample review: “Said 2 hours, it took 5. Couldn’t get anyone on the phone.” 2 stars.

We’re sorry for the long day and that you couldn’t reach us. We now text owners when their pet is ready and if anything runs long. – Sam

Why it works: Owns both problems with one clear fix.

5. Self-serve pet wash issue

Sample review: “Two of the wash stations had no warm water and the dryer wasn’t working.” 3 stars.

Thanks for letting us know, and we’re sorry your wash wasn’t easy. We’ve had the water heater and dryer serviced. If you’d like your wash credit back, just email us at hello@example.com.

Why it works: For a self-serve pet wash, fixing the equipment and saying so is the most important message.

6. Price complaint

Sample review: “$95 for a groom is ridiculous.” 3 stars.

Thank you for the feedback. Our price includes a bath, blowout, nail trim, ear cleaning, and a groomer who takes the time your pet needs. We understand it’s an investment, and we also offer bath-only and self-serve wash options at lower prices.

Why it works: It explains value and offers cheaper options without arguing.

7. Glowing groomer review

Sample review: “Bella looks like a show dog! Jenna was so gentle with her.” 5 stars.

Bella is a star! Thank you so much, and we’ll make sure Jenna sees this. We can’t wait to see her again.

Why it works: Using the pet’s name makes it personal and fun.

8. Pet wash praise

Sample review: “Best self-serve dog wash in town. Clean, warm water, great shampoo.” 5 stars.

Thank you! We work hard to keep every station clean and stocked. See you and your pup next bath day!

Why it works: Short, specific, and invites a return visit.

9. Daycare or boarding worry

Sample review: “Picked up my dog from boarding and he seemed lethargic.” 2 stars.

We’re sorry he seemed off, and we hope he’s back to himself. Many dogs are tired after boarding from extra play and excitement, but we always want to hear about any concern. Please call us at (555) 123-4567 so we can review his stay notes with you.

Why it works: Calm, caring, and offers to review records privately rather than in public.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Debating whether an injury was the groomer’s fault in public.
  • Blaming the pet’s behavior or the owner’s brushing habits.
  • Ignoring a safety or health concern instead of addressing it quickly.
  • Posting vet bill amounts or medical details in a reply.

Frequently asked questions

How should a groomer respond to a review about an injured pet?

Start with concern for the pet, acknowledge the issue, explain any process changes, and invite the owner to call the owner directly. Do not argue about the cause in public.

Should a pet wash respond to reviews about broken equipment?

Yes. Thank the reviewer, say the equipment has been fixed or serviced, and offer to make it right privately.

Is it okay to use the pet’s name in a reply?

Yes, if the owner used it in the review. It makes the reply feel personal.

How fast should pet businesses reply to reviews?

Within a day if a pet’s safety is mentioned, and within two days otherwise.
