Review response templates for every star rating

Copy-and-paste replies for small businesses · Updated October 29, 2026

Replying to every review shows future customers that you listen, but writing each reply from scratch takes time. These templates cover 1-star to 5-star reviews, star-only ratings, and reviews you think are fake. They work on Google, Yelp, and Facebook. Replace the bracketed parts with the customer’s name and a detail from their review so each reply sounds personal.

Three rules for any reply

  • Use the customer’s name and mention one specific detail from their review.
  • Keep it short. Two to four sentences is enough for most reviews.
  • For complaints, apologize once, skip the excuses, and give a direct way to reach you.

Template for a 5-star review

Hi [Name], thank you so much for the kind words! We’re really glad [detail they mentioned] made your day. It means a lot to our team, and we can’t wait to see you again.

Tip: Name one specific thing they praised so the reply doesn’t read like a form letter.

Template for a 4-star review

Thanks for the great review, [Name]! We’re happy you enjoyed [detail they liked]. If there’s anything that would have made it a five-star visit, we’d love to hear it. Just reply here or call us at [phone].

Tip: A four-star review is a happy customer with one small wish. Inviting that feedback shows you care about getting better.

Template for a 3-star review

Hi [Name], thank you for the honest feedback. We’re glad [positive detail] went well, and we’re sorry [issue they mentioned] fell short. We’re looking into it so it doesn’t happen again. If you’re willing, please reach out at [email or phone] so we can make your next visit better.

Tip: Mixed reviews deserve credit for the good part and a concrete fix for the rest.

Template for a 2-star review

Hi [Name], we’re sorry your experience didn’t meet expectations, especially [issue they mentioned]. That’s not the standard we hold ourselves to. I’d like to hear more and make it right. Please contact me directly at [email or phone]. [Your name], [role]

Tip: Sign it with a real name and role. It tells readers a person, not a bot, is taking responsibility.

Template for a 1-star review

Hi [Name], I’m truly sorry about [issue they mentioned]. You deserved better, and I’d like the chance to fix it. Please call or email me directly at [phone or email] so we can sort this out. [Your name], Owner

Tip: Stay calm, keep it short, skip the excuses, and move the conversation offline.

Template for a star rating with no text

Thank you for the [5-star] rating, [Name]! We appreciate you taking the time, and we look forward to seeing you again.

Tip: One sentence is plenty. For a low rating with no text, use: “We’re sorry we missed the mark. We’d love to hear what happened at [phone or email].”

Template for a review you think is fake

We take every review seriously, but we can’t find a record of a customer by this name or a visit matching this description. If we’ve missed something, please contact us at [phone or email] so we can look into it.

Tip: Stay polite, because future customers are reading. Then flag the review to the platform. See how to respond to fake reviews.

Questions owners ask

Is it OK to use the same template for every review?

Use a template as a starting point, not a final reply. Customers and Google can tell when every reply is identical, so swap in the customer’s name and one detail from their review each time.

How fast should I reply to a review?

Within a day or two is ideal. A quick reply to a negative review can stop it from becoming the first thing new customers notice.

Should I reply to reviews with no text?

Yes. A one-line thank-you to a star-only rating takes seconds and shows that you read every review.

Should I apologize in a reply to a negative review?

Apologize for the customer’s experience without arguing over details in public, then offer a direct way to reach you so you can make it right offline.
