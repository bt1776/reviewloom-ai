How to respond to a 2-star review

A practical guide for small-business owners · Updated November 10, 2026

A 2-star review is a disappointed customer, not a lost one. Unlike many 1-star reviews, it often mentions something that went right alongside the thing that went wrong, which means the reviewer was hoping to like you. A calm, specific reply can win them back, and it shows everyone reading your profile how you handle a mistake. For general advice, start with how to respond to Google reviews.

The short version

  • Thank them for taking the time to tell you.
  • Name the specific problem so they know you actually read it.
  • Apologize without excuses. One sentence of context at most.
  • Say what you're doing about it, but only if it's true.
  • Give a direct contact so the fix happens offline.

Why 2-star reviews deserve a fast reply

A 2-star reviewer is still on the fence. Many will update or follow up when an owner responds well, and the people reading later care less about the rating than about how you answered it. A defensive reply can turn a fixable bad visit into a reason for strangers to choose someone else, while a gracious one often earns you trust you didn't have before the review.

1. Good product, bad service

Thank you for the honest feedback, Jordan. We're glad you enjoyed the brisket, and we're sorry the 40-minute wait for your food took the shine off the night. That's not the pace we aim for, and we're adding a second cook on Saturday evenings. I'd love to make your next visit right. Please ask for me, Sam, or email owner@example.com.

Why it works: it credits the part they liked, names the exact problem, and gives a real change plus a person to ask for.

2. A repair or job that didn't hold

Thanks for letting us know, Pat, and I'm sorry the AC started blowing warm again a week after our visit. That shouldn't happen. Please call me directly at (555) 123-4567 and we'll come back out at no charge to find out what we missed.

Why it works: it doesn't argue about the cause in public. It owns the outcome and makes the next step easy.

3. The price felt unfair

Thank you for the review, Lee. We're sorry the final bill was higher than the estimate you expected. We should have called you before doing the extra work, and we've reminded our team to get approval first every time. I'd like to go over the invoice with you. Please reach out at service@example.com.

Why it works: it admits the real failure, which was the surprise, rather than defending the price itself.

4. A rude or rushed staff member

Thank you for telling us, Morgan, and I'm sorry you felt rushed at check-in. Everyone should feel welcome here, and I've shared your feedback with our front desk team. If you're open to it, I'd like to hear more. You can reach me at (555) 987-6543.

Why it works: it doesn't name or blame the employee in public, and it shows the feedback went somewhere. See more on reviews that mention employees.

5. Two stars with no explanation

Thanks for your review, Riley. We're sorry your visit didn't live up to what you hoped for. We'd really like to know what we could have done better, so please reach out at hello@example.com whenever you have a minute.

Why it works: it stays short and friendly, invites detail privately, and doesn't guess at a problem. For more, see reviews with no text.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Opening with "We're sorry you feel that way," which reads as an apology for their feelings rather than your mistake.
  • Explaining in detail why it wasn't really your fault.
  • Pasting the same apology under every low rating.
  • Offering a refund or discount in public.
  • Asking them to change or remove their review.
  • Sharing private details about their visit, order, or account.

What to do after you reply

Follow through on whatever you promised, because the reviewer will notice if you don't. If the same complaint shows up in more than one review, treat it as a pattern worth fixing. Questions owners ask

Should I respond to 2-star reviews?

Yes, and quickly. A 2-star reviewer is disappointed but usually not lost. A calm reply that owns the problem and offers a fix can win them back, and it shows every future reader how you handle mistakes.

How is a 2-star reply different from a 1-star reply?

A 2-star review usually mentions something that went right along with what went wrong. Acknowledge the good part briefly, then focus on the main problem. A 1-star review is often angrier, so it calls for even more care to stay calm and move the conversation offline.

Should I offer a refund or discount in my public reply?

Usually not in public. Offer to make it right and give a direct way to reach a manager or owner, then handle any refund or credit privately so the reply doesn't read like a coupon for complaining.

How long should a reply to a 2-star review be?

Three to five sentences. Thank them, name the specific problem, apologize without excuses, say what you're doing about it, and give a direct contact.
