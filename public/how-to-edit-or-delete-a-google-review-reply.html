How to edit or delete your reply to a Google review

For small business owners using Google Business Profile · Updated November 16, 2026

You posted a reply to a Google review and now want to change it, maybe there’s a typo, you replied while upset, the customer updated their review, or the problem has since been fixed. Good news: Google lets the business owner or manager edit or delete their reply at any time. This guide covers the steps, when it makes sense to update a reply, and example rewrites. For the basics of writing a reply in the first place, read how to respond to Google reviews.

The short version

  • Sign in with the Google account that owns or manages your Business Profile.
  • Open your reviews, find the review, and choose Edit to rewrite your reply or Delete to remove it.
  • You can only change your reply. You can’t edit or delete the customer’s review; only they can, or Google can remove it if it breaks policy.
  • Edited replies can take a few minutes to show publicly while Google checks them.

Steps on a computer (Google Search or Maps)

  1. Sign in to the Google account that manages your business.
  2. Search for your business name on Google, or open Google Maps. Your Business Profile management panel should appear.
  3. Select Read reviews (or Reviews).
  4. Find the review with the reply you want to change.
  5. Choose Edit to update the text and save, or Delete to remove the reply entirely.

Google moves buttons around from time to time. If you don’t see Edit next to your reply, look for a three-dot menu on the review.

Steps on a phone (Google Maps app)

  1. Open the Google Maps app signed in with the account that manages your business.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then your business profile, or tap the Business tab if you see it.
  3. Open Reviews and find the review.
  4. Tap the menu on your reply and choose Edit reply or Delete reply.

Should you edit, delete, or leave it?

Edit when the reply is mostly fine but has a typo, a wrong name, outdated contact info, or a tone you regret. Editing keeps a reply under the review, so readers still see you responded.

Delete and rewrite when the original reply was defensive, argued with the customer, shared private details, or could read as admitting legal fault. A fresh, calm reply is better than patching a bad one. See how to respond without admitting fault.

Leave it when the reply is professional and still accurate. Constant edits rarely help, and future customers mostly care that you responded calmly.

Avoid deleting a reply and leaving nothing. An unanswered negative review usually looks worse than a short, polite one.

When to update a reply

1. The customer updated their review

Original review: 2 stars about a long wait. Updated to 5 stars after you called and fixed it.

Thank you for giving us a second chance and for updating your review. We’re glad we could make it right, and we’ve changed how we schedule busy Saturdays so others don’t wait as long. See you next time! – Maria, Owner

Why it works: It replaces an apology that no longer fits with a thank-you that matches the new rating.

2. You replied while frustrated

Original reply: “That’s not what happened. You were the one who showed up late.”

Thank you for the feedback. I’m sorry your visit felt rushed. We want every appointment to feel unhurried, and I’d like to hear more about what happened. Please call me at (555) 123-4567 so we can talk it through. – Maria, Owner

Why it works: It drops the argument, acknowledges how the customer felt, and moves the details offline.

3. The problem has been fixed

Original reply promised to “look into” a broken booking link.

Thanks again for flagging this. Our online booking link was broken for a few days, and it’s fixed now. We appreciate you letting us know, and we hope to see you soon.

Why it works: Future readers see the issue is resolved, which is more reassuring than an open promise.

4. Contact info or staff changed

If an old reply lists a phone number, email, or manager who is no longer there, update it so customers don’t hit a dead end. This is a quick win on older reviews.

Common questions

Does the customer get notified when I edit my reply?

Google notifies reviewers when a business replies, and an edit may trigger another notice. Assume the customer may see your updated reply, and write it so you’re comfortable with that.

Why isn’t my edited reply showing?

Replies can take a few minutes, sometimes longer, to appear while Google checks them against its content policies. Replies with links, phone numbers, or heated language may be held longer or not shown.

Can I delete the customer’s review instead?

No. Only the reviewer can edit or delete their review. If it breaks Google’s policies, such as spam, a fake reviewer, or off-topic content, you can report it. See how to respond to fake reviews.

Who can edit replies on a Business Profile?

Owners and managers of the profile can reply, edit, and delete replies. If someone else on your team wrote a reply, any owner or manager can change it.
